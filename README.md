# Aqua Blue

## Rethinking the water in every shower.

Aqua Blue is a product development project focused on creating a new generation of compact shower water filtration products.

Our goal is to combine practical filtration, thoughtful product design, easy maintenance and reliable manufacturing into one everyday shower solution.

## The Concept

Aqua Blue is researching a new approach to shower water filtration, with a focus on:

- Practical filtration performance
- Compact product design
- Efficient water flow
- Easy installation
- Simple filter replacement
- Reliable manufacturing

The product is currently under development.

## Product Development

Our development process includes:

1. Research and concept validation
2. Filtration and materials research
3. Product engineering
4. Prototype development
5. Performance and durability testing
6. Manufacturer and supplier evaluation
7. Production preparation

## China & Manufacturing

Aqua Blue is exploring manufacturing and supplier opportunities in China, including filtration technologies, injection molding, components, packaging and consumer product manufacturing.

We aim to build long-term relationships with reliable manufacturing partners rather than simply selecting the lowest-cost supplier.
