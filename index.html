The concept

Better water starts with better thinking.

Shower water is often treated as something people simply have to accept. Aqua Blue is exploring a different approach: creating a compact filtration system designed specifically around the realities of everyday shower use.

Our goal is not to make another generic filter. We are researching how filtration performance, water flow, maintenance, installation and product design can work together in one system.

01 — PERFORMANCE

Filtration first

Researching practical filtration approaches that can improve shower water quality without unnecessarily restricting water flow.

02 — SIMPLICITY

Designed for everyday use

The product should be intuitive to install, simple to maintain and compatible with common shower setups.

03 — ENGINEERING

Form follows function

Every design decision is evaluated against performance, durability, manufacturability and the real conditions of daily use.

The problem

Shower filtration has room to evolve.

Existing solutions can be difficult to evaluate, inconvenient to maintain or designed around compromises between filtration, flow and usability. Aqua Blue is investigating how those compromises can be reduced.

≋

Water quality

Shower water can contain substances that influence its smell, appearance or overall user experience. The right filtration technology needs to address specific water conditions rather than make vague promises.

◇

Product design

A filtration product should feel like part of the shower, not an awkward accessory attached to it.

↻

Maintenance

Filter replacement and routine maintenance should be straightforward, predictable and easy to understand.

Product development

From research to a real product.

Aqua Blue is following a structured development process. The product will only move forward when its performance, usability and manufacturing requirements have been validated.

PHASE 01

Research

Study existing filtration technologies, shower systems, customer needs and market gaps.

PHASE 02

Engineering

Develop the product architecture, filtration system, materials and connection concept.

PHASE 03

Prototyping

Build prototypes and evaluate flow, durability, filtration performance and usability.

PHASE 04

Manufacturing

Work with qualified manufacturing partners to refine tooling, quality control and production requirements.

China & manufacturing

Building the right manufacturing network.

China offers a deep manufacturing ecosystem for filtration, plastics, precision components, packaging and consumer products. Aqua Blue is exploring supplier relationships and production capabilities as the product moves through development.

Supplier research

Identifying manufacturers with relevant experience in water filtration, injection molding, seals, fittings and consumer hardware.

Product sourcing

Evaluating materials, filtration media, components and production methods with an emphasis on consistency and long-term reliability.

Quality control

Developing clear specifications and inspection criteria before moving toward larger production volumes.

Long-term cooperation

The objective is to establish reliable supplier relationships rather than simply find the lowest-cost manufacturer.
