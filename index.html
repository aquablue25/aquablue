The Concept

Better water starts with better filtration thinking.

Shower water passes through a compact system in seconds. That makes material selection, flow resistance, contact time, cartridge geometry and service life critical parts of the design.


Aqua Blue is exploring different filtration media and manufacturing approaches to develop a practical shower filtration platform.

Filtration Media

Candidate technologies under evaluation.

Different material classes solve different engineering problems. The objective is not to use every material, but to identify the combination that delivers the best balance of filtration, flow, durability and manufacturability.

Fibrous media

PP / PET / Nonwoven

Nonwoven and synthetic fibre structures can be engineered for particle retention, drainage and support.

Surface area

Pleated filter media

Pleating increases effective filtration area within a compact cartridge geometry and can be evaluated for pressure-drop and dirt-holding performance.

Adsorption

Activated carbon

Activated carbon is widely used as an adsorption medium. Performance depends strongly on carbon type, surface area, loading and water contact conditions.

Media chemistry

KDF-type media

Copper-zinc media such as KDF-type formulations are commonly evaluated in water-treatment applications and can be combined with other media.

Fine filtration

Membrane technology

Membrane-based structures can provide finer separation, but they introduce additional considerations such as pressure, flow resistance, fouling and maintenance.

Structure

Mesh, cage & support

Meshes, support cores, cages, end caps, seals and other structural components keep the filtration system stable during operation.

Manufacturing

From raw material to finished cartridge.

A scalable filtration product depends not only on the media, but also on converting, pleating, cutting, bonding, assembly, sealing and quality-control processes.

Production

Production lines

Automated and semi-automated systems can support repeatable cutting, forming and assembly operations.

Converting

Pleating & cutting

Pleating geometry, fold depth and material tension can affect effective area, flow and pressure drop.

Quality

Testing & analysis

Prototype cartridges can be evaluated before production using controlled flow, pressure and water-quality testing.

Testing & Analysis

Performance has to be measured.

Final material selection should be based on controlled testing, not simply on the number of filtration stages or the appearance of a cartridge.

F

Flow rate

Measure how the cartridge affects shower flow under realistic operating conditions.

ΔP

Pressure drop

Determine how much resistance the filtration architecture introduces as water moves through the system.

C

Target compounds

Evaluate reduction of specifically targeted substances using appropriate laboratory methods.

L

Service life

Study how filtration performance changes with cumulative water volume and operating time.

Development note: These materials and architectures are candidate technologies, not confirmed Aqua Blue performance claims. Final media selection should be determined through laboratory testing and target-water analysis.
