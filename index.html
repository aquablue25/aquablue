The problem

Water is part of the shower experience.

Aqua Blue is investigating how the water that reaches the shower can be approached differently — from filtration architecture and materials to product design and everyday usability.

01 / EVERYDAY WATER

The shower is where water meets you.

The goal is not simply to add another bathroom accessory. We are exploring the complete experience of filtered shower water.

02 / PRODUCT THINKING

Filtration should not compromise design.

Aqua Blue is being developed around the idea that filtration, maintenance, installation and aesthetics should work together.

The water journey

What happens between the pipe and the shower?

The exact Aqua Blue filtration architecture is still being researched. This conceptual model shows how we are thinking about the product — as a controlled water journey rather than a single component.

Designed around the flow.

Water enters the system, passes through different filtration stages and exits through the shower connection. Each stage can be researched independently before the final architecture is defined.

STAGE 01

Water enters

Incoming shower water enters the filtration housing through a standard connection.

STAGE 02

Initial filtration

A first stage can be designed to capture larger particles and protect downstream media.

STAGE 03

Treatment media

Different filtration media can be evaluated during research and prototype testing.

STAGE 04

Final stage

The final stage is being considered as part of the overall flow, pressure and usability requirements.

STAGE 05

Shower outlet

The treated water continues into the existing shower system.

01 / PROTECTION

Protect the system

The first stage can help manage incoming particulate matter and protect the internal filtration media.

02 / MEDIA

Research the right media

Aqua Blue will evaluate filtration materials according to performance, flow requirements, durability and manufacturing feasibility.

03 / FLOW

Preserve the shower experience

04 / DESIGN

Make the technology disappear

Product thinking

Engineering meets everyday design.

A successful shower filtration product has to work beyond the filter media itself. Installation, replacement, materials, dimensions and appearance are all part of the development process.

PRODUCT

A compact system designed for real bathrooms.

The form factor will be developed around standard shower connections, usability and the visual language of modern bathroom products.

WATER

The water itself becomes part of the design.

Aqua Blue is exploring visual and technical ways to communicate the journey of water through the product.
